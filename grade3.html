GRAMMAR CORE

✍️ Present Simple

ვისწავლოთ მოქმედებები, რომლებიც ხშირად ხდება.

⚡

I / You / We / They

I play football.
You like music.
We study English.
They live in Georgia.

Rule: I / You / We / They + verb
⭐

He / She / It

He plays football.
She likes music.
It works well.

Rule: He / She / It + verb + S
