✏️ Grammar Lab

Simple grammar designed for Grade 3 learners.

🔵 Present Simple

We use the Present Simple for things that happen regularly or are generally true.

Person Example
I / You / We / They I play football.
He / She / It She plays football.

🟣 Have / Has

Use have with I, you, we and they. Use has with he, she and it.

  • I have a blue bag.
  • They have two dogs.
  • She has a new book.
  • He has a bicycle.

🟢 There is / There are

Use There is for one thing and There are for more than one.

  • There is a cat on the chair.
  • There are three books on the desk.
  • There is a tree in the garden.

🟡 Can / Can't

We use can to talk about ability.

  • I can swim.
  • She can sing.
  • We can run fast.
  • A fish can't fly.

🚀 Mia's Amazing Saturday

Mia wakes up early on Saturday morning. The sun is shining and the sky is blue. She puts on her yellow jacket and walks to the park with her brother Alex.

In the park they see a small brown dog. The dog is running after a red ball. Mia throws the ball and the dog runs very fast. Alex laughs and says, "What a funny dog!"

After the park, Mia and Alex go home. Their mother is making pancakes in the kitchen. They eat breakfast together and talk about their day. Mia says, "Saturday is my favorite day!"
