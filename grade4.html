✍️ Present Simple

დადებითი: I play. / You play. / He plays. / She plays.
უარყოფითი: I do not play. / He does not play.
კითხვა: Do you play? / Does he play?
📌 He/She/It-თან ზმნას ემატება -s/-es.
Frequency: always, usually, often, sometimes, never.
I usually read books. — მე ჩვეულებრივ წიგნებს ვკითხულობ.

❓ Questions

What — რა / Who — ვინ / Where — სად / When — როდის / Why — რატომ / How — როგორ
What do you like? — რა მოგწონს?
Where do you live? — სად ცხოვრობ?
When do you study? — როდის სწავლობ?
Why do you learn English? — რატომ სწავლობ ინგლისურს?

📖 Reading

A Busy School Day

Emma gets up early every morning. She has breakfast and goes to school by bus. At school she studies English, science and maths. She usually reads during break time. After school she goes home, does her homework and helps her family. In the evening she reads a book and goes to bed at ten.
