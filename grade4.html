⚙️ Grammar Command Center

Master the grammar structures you need for Grade 4.

🟦 Present Simple

Use it for routines, habits and facts.

I go to school every day.
She likes music.

🟪 Present Continuous

Use it for actions happening now.

I am reading now.
They are playing.

🟨 Past Simple

Use it for completed actions in the past.

We visited the museum yesterday.
He played football.

🟩 Future

Talk about plans and future actions.

I will help you.
We are going to travel.

🔵 Comparatives

Compare two people, animals or things.

A lion is stronger than a cat.

🟣 Questions

Learn question words and sentence structure.

Where do you live?
Why are you happy?

🚀 The Secret Science Club

Mia and Daniel are members of a small science club at their school. Every Friday, they meet in the school laboratory and work on a new project.

One afternoon, they decide to build a small robot. Daniel brings some old electronic parts, while Mia writes a simple computer program.

After two hours, the robot suddenly moves across the table. The students are surprised and excited. Their teacher smiles and says, “Great work! You never know what you can discover when you work together.”

Mia writes the final result in her notebook. She knows that this is only the beginning of their next big adventure.

🎯 Mission Questions

  1. Where do Mia and Daniel meet?
  2. What do they build?
  3. What does Daniel bring?
  4. What does Mia write?
  5. Why are the students excited?
