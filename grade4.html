🧠 Grammar Control Center

📘 Present Simple

I / You / We / They
I play football.
You play football.
We play football.
They play football.

He / She / It
He plays football.
She reads books.
It works well.

📌 He / She / It-თან ზმნას ხშირად ემატება -s / -es.

❓ Do / Does

Do you like English?
Yes, I do.
No, I don't.

Does she play tennis?
Yes, she does.
No, she doesn't.

📌 Do → I / You / We / They
📌 Does → He / She / It

⏳ Was / Were

I was tired yesterday.
He was at school.
She was happy.

We were at home.
They were busy.

💪 Can

I can swim.
She can sing.
He can ride a bike.

Can you speak English?
Yes, I can.
No, I can't.

🎒 Have / Has

I have a book.
You have a bike.
We have a house.

He has a dog.
She has a red bag.
It has four legs.

🏠 There is / There are

There is a book on the table.
There is a school near my house.

There are three pencils.
There are two cats in the room.

📖 Reading Mission

A Busy School Day

Emma gets up early every morning. She has breakfast and goes to school by bus. At school she studies English, science and maths. She usually reads during break time. After school she goes home, does her homework and helps her family. In the evening she reads a book and goes to bed at ten.
