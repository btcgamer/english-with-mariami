📚 GRAMMAR LAB

ძირითადი გრამატიკა

Present Simple

I play football.
You like English.
We go to school.
They read books.

He / She / It

He plays football.
She likes music.
It works well.

To Be

I am a student.
You are my friend.
He is happy.
They are ready.

Have / Has

I have a book.
We have a house.
She has a cat.
He has a bike.

Can / Can't

I can swim.
She can read.
He can't fly.
We can't stop.

There is / There are

There is a book.
There is a school.
There are two cats.
There are many students.
