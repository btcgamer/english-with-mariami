## L1 — Hello!
**Topic:** Greetings & Introductions  
**Grammar:** be — I am / You are / He is / She is  
**Vocabulary:** hello, hi, goodbye, name, friend, boy, girl, teacher

**Reading**
> Hello! My name is Nino. I am eight. This is my friend, Luka. He is eight, too. We are in class. Our teacher is Mariami. She is our teacher. Goodbye!

**Listening**
> Teacher: Hello! What is your name?  
> Nino: I am Nino.  
> Teacher: Hi, Nino. This is Luka.  
> Nino: Hello, Luka!  
> Luka: Hi! I am Luka.  
> Teacher: You are friends. Great!

**Speaking:** Say your name. Say one friend's name. Say who the teacher is.  
**Exercises:** choose am/is/are; match greeting to situation; complete a 3-line introduction; identify Nino/Luka from the dialogue.  
**Quiz:** 6 questions: be forms + speaker/name identification.

## L2 — My Family
**Topic:** Family  
**Grammar:** have got / has got  
**Vocabulary:** mother, father, sister, brother, grandmother, grandfather, baby, family

**Reading**
> This is my family. I have got a mother and a father. I have got one sister. Her name is Ana. My brother has got a small red ball. My grandmother and grandfather live near us. We are a happy family.

**Listening**
> Ana: Have you got a brother?  
> Nika: Yes, I have got a brother. His name is Gio.  
> Ana: Has Gio got a sister?  
> Nika: Yes. He has got me!

**Speaking:** Describe 3–4 family members using have got/has got.  
**Exercises:** family matching; have/has choice; complete sentences; answer relationship questions.  
**Quiz:** identify family relationships and select correct have/has form.

## L3 — Colors
**Topic:** Colors Around Me  
**Grammar:** It is + color  
**Vocabulary:** red, blue, green, yellow, black, white, orange, pink, purple

**Reading**
> Look around the classroom. The board is green. The door is brown. A pencil is yellow. A bag is blue. The book is red. Maria has a pink pencil case. She says, “It is pink!” We can find many colors at school.

**Listening**
> Mariami: Find the ball. What color is it?  
> Child: It is orange.  
> Mariami: Good. Now find the book.  
> Child: It is blue.  
> Mariami: And the pencil?  
> Child: It is yellow.

**Speaking:** Point to four objects and say their colors.  
**Exercises:** color/object matching; complete “It is ___”; color hunt; choose the object matching a color clue.  
**Quiz:** object-color matching and sentence completion.

## L4 — Numbers 1–20
**Topic:** How many?  
**Grammar:** How many? + numbers 1–20  
**Vocabulary:** one–twenty, count, more, less

**Reading**
> Leo has a box of toys. He counts six cars and four balls. He has ten toys. His sister has eight toys. Leo has more toys than his sister. They count the toys again together: one, two, three, four, five, six...

**Listening**
> Teacher: How many pencils are there?  
> Child: There are twelve pencils.  
> Teacher: How many books?  
> Child: There are seven books.  
> Teacher: Which number is more, twelve or seven?  
> Child: Twelve.

**Speaking:** Count objects and answer “How many?”  
**Exercises:** number-word matching; missing-number sequence; count pictures; more/less choice.  
**Quiz:** number recognition, quantity answer, one-step more/less reasoning.

## L5 — My Body
**Topic:** My Body  
**Grammar:** This is my...  
**Vocabulary:** head, face, eye, ear, nose, mouth, hand, arm, leg, foot

**Reading**
> This is my body. This is my head. This is my face. I have two eyes and two ears. I have one nose and one mouth. These are my hands. These are my legs and feet. I use my eyes to see.

**Listening**
> Child: What is this?  
> Teacher: This is your nose.  
> Child: And this?  
> Teacher: This is your ear.  
> Child: What are these?  
> Teacher: These are your hands.

**Speaking:** Point to and name 6–8 body parts.  
**Exercises:** label body picture; match word to body part; complete “This is my ___”; identify a body part from a clue.  
**Quiz:** body-part identification and this/these choice.

## L6 — Animals
**Topic:** Animals  
**Grammar:** I like... / I see...  
**Vocabulary:** cat, dog, bird, fish, horse, cow, lion, elephant, monkey

**Reading**
> We are at the animal park. I see a big elephant. I see a monkey in a tree. My friend sees a lion. I like the elephant. My friend likes the monkey. We also see birds and a small fish pond.

**Listening**
> Luka: What do you see?  
> Nino: I see a horse and two cows.  
> Luka: Do you like cows?  
> Nino: Yes, I like cows. I also see a dog.  
> Luka: I like dogs!

**Speaking:** Say three animals you see and two animals you like.  
**Exercises:** animal matching/classification; complete I like/I see; park-picture identification; choose animal from clue.  
**Quiz:** identify animals and speaker preferences.

## L7 — Food I Like
**Topic:** Food Preferences  
**Grammar:** I like / I don't like  
**Vocabulary:** apple, banana, bread, cheese, rice, soup, egg, cake, pizza, milk

**Reading**
> Mia is talking about food. She likes apples, bananas and pizza. She likes milk, too. She does not like soup. Her brother likes soup and rice, but he does not like cheese. They have different food preferences.

**Listening**
> Teacher: Do you like pizza?  
> Sam: Yes, I like pizza.  
> Teacher: Do you like soup?  
> Sam: No, I don't like soup.  
> Teacher: What about milk?  
> Sam: I like milk.

**Speaking:** Give three likes and two dislikes.  
**Exercises:** food matching; like/don't like choice; class preference survey; complete a short preference chart.  
**Quiz:** identify a speaker's likes/dislikes and choose the correct sentence.

## L8 — My Toys
**Topic:** Toys  
**Grammar:** This is my... / These are my...  
**Vocabulary:** ball, doll, car, kite, robot, teddy bear, blocks, toy, my, these

**Reading**
> This is my toy box. This is my red car. This is my robot. These are my blocks. These are my two balls. My teddy bear is next to the box. I like my toys. My sister has a doll.

**Listening**
> Ana: What is this?  
> Nika: This is my robot.  
> Ana: And what are these?  
> Nika: These are my blocks.  
> Ana: Is the red car yours?  
> Nika: Yes, it is my car.

**Speaking:** Describe four toys using this/these and my.  
**Exercises:** singular/plural sorting; this/these choice; possessive matching; toy-box description.  
**Quiz:** identify whose toy it is and choose singular/plural form.

## L9 — At School
**Topic:** Classroom Objects  
**Grammar:** a / an  
**Vocabulary:** book, pencil, pen, ruler, bag, eraser, desk, chair, school, classroom

**Reading**
> I am in my classroom. I have a book and a pencil on my desk. I have an eraser in my bag. There is a ruler next to my book. My friend has a pen and a school bag. We are ready for class.

**Listening**
> Teacher: What is on your desk?  
> Child: A book and a pencil.  
> Teacher: What is in your bag?  
> Child: An eraser and a ruler.  
> Teacher: Good. Please take a pen.

**Speaking:** Name five classroom objects using a/an.  
**Exercises:** a/an choice; object labeling; find the missing object; complete short classroom sentences.  
**Quiz:** a/an in context plus classroom-object comprehension.

## L10 — Weather
**Topic:** Weather  
**Grammar:** It is...  
**Vocabulary:** sunny, rainy, cloudy, windy, snowy, hot, cold, weather, today

**Reading**
> Good morning! Here is today's weather report. It is sunny and warm in the morning. It is windy in the afternoon. There is no rain today. Tomorrow may be cloudy. Take your light jacket because the afternoon is cool.

**Listening**
> Good morning. Today it is rainy in Batumi. It is cloudy in Kutaisi. In Tbilisi, it is sunny but windy. Take an umbrella in Batumi. Have a nice day!

**Speaking:** Give a 3–4 sentence weather report for today.  
**Exercises:** weather-picture matching; complete “It is ___”; choose suitable clothing/item; create a mini weather report.  
**Quiz:** identify weather from clues and choose the correct weather sentence.
