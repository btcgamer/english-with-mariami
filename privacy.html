English with Mariami — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 November 2026

1. What this app does

English with Mariami is an educational English-learning application for students in Grades 1–12. It provides lessons, vocabulary, grammar, listening, speaking, reading, writing, quizzes and learning progress.

2. Information we process

When an account is created or used, the service may process an email address and an internal user identifier. For student accounts, it may also store the assigned grade and learning information such as completed lessons, XP, stars and streak/progress data.

The app may use local device storage for learning state and preferences.

3. Why we use this information

  • Authenticate users and keep accounts secure.
  • Provide the correct grade and lessons.
  • Save and display learning progress and rewards.
  • Operate, troubleshoot and improve the educational service.

Subscription purchases

When web payments are enabled, Lemon Squeezy processes card payments on its hosted checkout. The service sends your signed-in email address, an internal account identifier and a checkout reference to associate a purchase with your learning account. Our protected payment records retain subscription identifiers, the selected period, test/live mode, subscription status, expiry, refund status and event hashes. We check the payment provider before granting paid access. Full payment-card details are entered with the payment provider and are not collected or stored by this application. See Lemon Squeezy's privacy policy.

When Google Play subscriptions are enabled, Google processes those payments. The service verifies and retains purchase tokens and related account/verification records for restoration and access checks.

Cancel a subscription through the provider where you purchased it: the web payment management portal available from the subscription page, or Google Play. Deleting a learning account does not by itself cancel renewal. Payment providers may keep transaction records according to their own legal and retention requirements. For privacy or deletion requests, contact razmadze467@gmail.com.

4. Service providers

The application uses Supabase for authentication and application data storage. Other platform services may process technical information required to deliver the app. We do not sell personal information.

5. Children and students

The app is designed for educational use by students, including children. Where required, a parent, guardian, school or other authorized adult should create or manage a child's account and provide any required consent. Users should not submit unnecessary personal information.

6. Data retention and deletion

Account and learning data may be retained while an account is active or as needed to provide the service. Account/data deletion requests can be made through the service operator. Contact razmadze467@gmail.com for account/data deletion requests.

7. Security

We use authentication controls and database access rules intended to limit access to account and learning data. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated when the service, data practices or legal requirements change.

9. Contact

For support or privacy questions, email razmadze467@gmail.com. Users should not send passwords or sensitive credentials through support channels.
