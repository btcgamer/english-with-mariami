# Vocabulary Audit — Grades 2–4

## Purpose
A safe planning report for vocabulary progression. This document does not modify lesson data, IDs, progress, authentication, Supabase, or PWA behavior.

## Progression rule
Vocabulary may repeat when it reinforces a core word in a new context. Repetition should be considered accidental only when the same word is reused without a new task, sentence pattern, context, or communication goal.

## Grade 2 target
Concrete, high-frequency vocabulary with strong visual support: animals, numbers, colors, family, food, home, weather, clothes, school, transport, places, and space.

## Grade 3 target
Move from naming to using words in short sentences and practical contexts. Prioritize sentence building, routines, places/directions, descriptions, simple reading/listening, and short speaking responses.

## Grade 4 target
Move from isolated vocabulary to contextual meaning and communication. Prioritize school/learning, routines, weather/seasons, city/travel, reading/listening context, opinions, shopping dialogue, directions, word relationships, and integrated assessment.

## Intentional reinforcement
- Family, food, home, school, weather, places, transport, and space can recur across grades when sentence complexity increases.
- Core verbs and everyday words should recur because learners need retrieval practice.
- Grade 4 should reuse selected Grade 3 words inside longer sentences, dialogues, and comprehension tasks rather than simply re-teaching word lists.

## Watch list
- Avoid adding large synonym lists simply to make Grade 4 look harder.
- Avoid replacing familiar Grade 2 words when the goal is fluency.
- Avoid identical vocabulary exercises across grades.
- Check that new Grade 4 words are used in sentences, reading/listening, or speaking tasks.

## Next implementation gate
Before changing vocabulary, inspect the exact vocabulary arrays/content in each Grade. Only then classify items as reinforcement, accidental duplication, or missing progression.
