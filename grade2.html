SMART GRAMMAR

✏️ Grammar Lab

🔵 To Be

I am • You are • He is • She is

I am happy.
You are my friend.
He is a student.
She is nice.

🟣 Have / Has

I have • He has

I have a book.
You have a pencil.
She has a cat.
He has a bike.

🟢 Present Simple

I play • He plays

I play football.
She likes apples.
He reads books.

🟡 Questions

What? Where? Who? How?

What is your name?
Where do you live?
Who is she?
How are you?

🚀 My Little English Day

Hello! My name is Alex. I am eight years old. I live in a small house with my family. I have a mother, a father and a little sister. Every morning I wake up, wash my face and eat breakfast. Then I go to school. I love my teacher and my friends. We read books, write words and play games. After school I go home. I do my homework and play with my dog. In the evening I eat dinner with my family. Then I read a book and go to bed. I love my happy day!
