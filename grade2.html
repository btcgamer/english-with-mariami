✏️ Grade 2 Grammar

Simple grammar rules with easy examples.

👤 I / You / We / They

I am happy.

You are nice.

We are friends.

They are students.

👦 He / She / It

He is tall.

She is kind.

It is small.

🔵 Have / Has

I have a book.

You have a dog.

She has a cat.

He has a bike.

🏃 Can

I can run.

She can swim.

He can jump.

They can dance.

🚫 Can't

I can't fly.

A fish can't walk.

A bird can't drive.

🔴 A / An

a dog

a book

an apple

an orange

📍 In / On / Under

The ball is in the box.

The book is on the table.

The cat is under the chair.

🔢 Plurals

one cat → two cats

one dog → two dogs

one book → three books

one box → two boxes

❓ Question Words

What? What is it?

Who? Who is she?

Where? Where is the cat?

When? When is your birthday?

How? How are you?

📚 Reading 1 — My Family

My name is Anna. I am eight years old. I have a small family. My mother is kind and my father is funny. I have one brother. His name is Tom. We have a little dog. His name is Max. I love my family very much.

📚 Reading 2 — At School

I go to school every day. I have a teacher and many friends. In my school bag I have a book, a pencil, a ruler and a notebook. I like English because it is fun. We read, write, speak and play games.

📚 Reading 3 — A Sunny Day

Today is sunny and warm. The sky is blue and the sun is bright. I go to the park with my friend. We play football and ride our bikes. Then we eat apples and drink water. It is a very happy day.
