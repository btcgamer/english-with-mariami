✏️ GRAMMAR LAB

Grade 2 Grammar

მარტივი წესები და მაგალითები.

🔵 I am / You are

I am happy.
You are my friend.
I am a student.

🟢 This is / That is

This is a book.
That is a house.
This is my dog.

🟡 I have / I like

I have a cat.
I like milk.
I have a red ball.

🟣 Can / Can't

I can swim.
I can read.
I can't fly.

🟠 Plurals

one cat → two cats
one book → three books
one dog → four dogs

🔴 Questions

What is this?
Who is she?
Where is the cat?
