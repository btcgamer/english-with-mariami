# English with Mariami — Curriculum Audit Report

## Scope
Cross-grade planning audit for Grades 2–4. Diagnostic only; no lesson, progress, authentication, Supabase, or PWA behavior is changed by this report.

## Verified Grade 4 structure
Grade 4 contains 24 lessons across 6 worlds:

1. Grammar Core — Present Simple; Do/Does Questions; Don't/Doesn't; There is/There are
2. Grammar Advanced — Was/Were; Prepositions; Can/Can't; Adjectives & Adverbs
3. Vocabulary Galaxy — School & Learning; Daily Routine; Weather & Seasons; City & Travel
4. Reading & Listening — The Science Museum; The Helpful Robot; Weekend Adventure; Future City
5. Speaking & Listening Lab — My Day; My Opinion; At the Shop; Directions
6. Brain Games & Final — Grammar Quiz Arena; Vocabulary Vault; Sentence Factory; Champion Mission

## Quality rules for the next content pass
- Keep Grade 2 concrete and vocabulary-first.
- Make Grade 3 the bridge from vocabulary to sentence use, comprehension, and short communication.
- Make Grade 4 extend Grade 3 through connected sentences, functional dialogue, reading/listening comprehension, and supported speaking.
- Do not add a topic only because it sounds advanced; add it only when the existing lesson evidence shows a real learning gap.
- Preserve existing progress IDs and Supabase/auth behavior when editing lesson content.

## Confirmed G4 strengths
- Grammar progression is explicit and grouped before advanced practice.
- Vocabulary is organized into school, routine, weather/seasons, and city/travel contexts.
- Reading/listening provides short contextual texts rather than isolated words.
- Speaking includes opinion, shopping, and directions for functional English.
- Final activities integrate grammar, vocabulary, sentence building, and assessment.

## Next audit targets
1. Compare exact vocabulary overlap between adjacent grades.
2. Check whether repeated words are intentional reinforcement or accidental duplication.
3. Check sentence difficulty progression, not just topic names.
4. Check that every G4 reading/listening lesson has comprehension work tied to its text.
5. Check that speaking lessons require production, not only repetition.
6. Check final assessment coverage against all G4 grammar and communication targets.

## Safety gate
No automatic content rewrite should occur from this report. Any content change must be made only after the corresponding real lesson source is inspected and should preserve existing IDs/state contracts.
